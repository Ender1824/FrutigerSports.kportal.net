Pick your sport

Every game reads how you move your phone — the angle, the speed, the twist of your wrist — so a little practice really pays off.

Table Tennis

Your phone is the paddle.

  • Tilt the phone for forehand or backhand, and swing as the ball reaches you.
  • Brush up for topspin, chop down to slice, twist to curve it.
  • Point the paddle face to aim — a line shows where your shot will land.
  • Play the computer, take the Return Challenge, or face a friend nearby.

Golf

Swing it like a club.

  • Take the club back to set your power, then swing through.
  • Turn your wrist at impact to hook or slice around the trees.
  • Read the wind, pick your club, and watch the shot line bend.
  • Three holes at Palm Cove, or Closest to the Pin.

Bowling

Grip, swing, let go.

  • Hold the screen to grip the ball, swing your arm, and let go to roll.
  • Turn your wrist as you release — like turning a doorknob — to hook it.
  • Line up with Move and Angle, then watch the pins fly.
  • Ten frames, or Spin Control with barriers to curve around.

Archery

Draw back, hold steady.

  • Raise your phone like a bow and pull back to draw.
  • The sight closes in as your aim settles — don't hold too long or it resets.
  • Allow for the arrow's drop and the wind, then let go.
  • Beginner, Intermediate and Expert courses, with secret targets to find.
