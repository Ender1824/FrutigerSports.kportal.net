Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026

The short version: Frutiger Sports doesn't collect your personal information. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your profile and scores are stored only on your iPhone, and the app never sends anything to our servers — it doesn't use any.

This policy explains how the Frutiger Sports app for iPhone (“the app”) and this website handle information. If you have any questions, email universe@kportal.net.

Information we collect

None. The app has no sign‑in, doesn't connect to the internet, and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking tools. We don't sell or share personal information, because we don't have any.

Information stored on your device

To remember you between games, the app saves the following on your iPhone only:

  • Your player profile: the name you choose (it starts as “Player” and a number), your character's look and your favourite colour.
  • Your settings, such as control style, sensitivity, difficulty, music, sound and haptics.
  • Your game results and skill levels for each sport.

This information never leaves your device except as described under Play Nearby below. It may be included in your iPhone's own backups (for example iCloud Backup), according to your device settings. Deleting the app deletes this information.

Motion sensors

The app reads your iPhone's motion sensors (accelerometer and gyroscope) while you play, to turn your movements into swings, throws and aiming. These readings are used instantly on your device and are never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

Play Nearby (local network)

Play Nearby lets two iPhones on the same Wi‑Fi network play together. It uses your local network only — nothing passes through the internet or any server.

  • When you host or look for a game, your player name, character look, favourite colour and chosen sport are visible to other devices running Frutiger Sports on the same Wi‑Fi network.
  • During a match, the two phones exchange game information directly, such as shots, scores and your profile, so each player can see the other.
  • Nothing exchanged is stored after the match, apart from your own results on your own phone.

iOS asks for your permission before the app can use the local network. You can say no and still play every sport on your own, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network.

Crash reports from Apple

If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings (Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements), Apple may give us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. Apple handles that data under its own privacy policy, and it doesn't identify you to us.

Children

Frutiger Sports is suitable for all ages. Because the app doesn't collect personal information from anyone, it doesn't collect personal information from children.

This website

This website doesn't use cookies, analytics, trackers or third‑party content. Like any website, our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses and the pages requested) for security and reliability.

Your choices and rights

You can view and change your profile and settings in the app at any time, and you can remove all of the app's data by deleting the app. Since we don't hold any of your information, there's nothing for us to access, correct or delete — but if you have a question or request, we're happy to help.

Changes to this policy

If the way the app handles information changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above before the change takes effect.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email universe@kportal.net.
