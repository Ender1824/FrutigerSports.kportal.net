Common questions

How should I hold my phone?

Hold it firmly, like the real thing, with the screen facing you. In table tennis it's the paddle handle (the back of the phone is the hitting face); in golf it's the grip of the club; in bowling it's the ball in your hand; in archery you hold it up like a bow. Make sure you have room around you, and keep a good grip when you swing.

The paddle doesn't point where I expect in table tennis.

Press and hold the screen to re‑center: however you're holding the phone at that moment becomes the neutral position. Tilt the top of the phone right for a forehand and left for a backhand.

The controls feel too sensitive (or not sensitive enough).

Open Settings in the app and adjust the Sensitivity slider. You can also change the Difficulty there.

Can I play without swinging my phone?

Yes. In the app's Settings, set Controls to Touch, and every sport switches to swipes, drags and taps on the screen.

Play Nearby can't find the other player.

Both iPhones need to be on the same Wi‑Fi network, with Frutiger Sports allowed to use the local network (Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network). On both phones, open the same sport and choose Play Nearby; one player taps Host and the other joins. Some public or guest Wi‑Fi networks stop devices from seeing each other — if that happens, try a home network or a personal hotspot.

How do I turn off the music, sound effects or vibration?

In the app's Settings, under Feedback, switch Music, Sound effects or Haptics off.

How do I start over?

Your profile and scores are stored only on your phone. Deleting the app removes them; reinstall it for a fresh start.
